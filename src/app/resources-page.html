How this page works

  1. Pick a resource from the list. Each one is a kind of record FHIR defines, like a patient, a lab result or a claim.
  2. Its card starts with one plain sentence and, where it helps, a few things that are good to know.
  3. Schema shows its key fields: the field's name, its type, how many there can be (0..1 optional, 1..1 required, 0..* a list) and what it holds. Show all fields reveals the rest, and ▸ opens a group of nested fields.
  4. Sample shows the official FHIR example in JSON, trimmed to the same fields.
